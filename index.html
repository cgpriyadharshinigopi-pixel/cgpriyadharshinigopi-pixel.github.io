Prompt Template

Reusable K–12 concept prompt

ROLE: K–12 Science educator specialising in Grade 8 Physical Science TASK: Generate a concept explanation for [TOPIC] CONSTRAINTS: – Simple language for a 13–14 year old student – Avoid jargon unless it's curriculum vocabulary — define it immediately – Length: 180–220 words – No bullet points — flowing paragraphs only – Address a common student misconception TONE: Friendly, clear, encouraging — like a great teacher explaining for the first time OUTPUT FORMAT: ¶1 Real-world hook ¶2 Core concept explained simply ¶3 Equation/process explained step by step ¶4 Common misconception addressed ¶5 One-sentence summary

QC rubric I designed

Accuracy ................ 0–3 Clarity .................. 0–3 Grade-level fit .......... 0–2 Misconception addressed .. 0–1 CK-12 FlexBook alignment . 0–1 TOTAL .................... /10
Before → After Prompt Refinement

Initial: "Explain photosynthesis for students."

Issues identified: no grade-level target, output used bullet points and headers despite needing flowing paragraphs, no misconception check, no word-count control.

After: A structured Grade 8 prompt with explicit tone, word count, equation, misconception, and output requirements.

Project Evidence
Before/after comparison showing the initial AI output, issues identified, refined output, and QC checklist.
What I evaluated

Accuracy

Scientific claims, equation balance, and correctness of the explanation.

Grade-level fit

Vocabulary, sentence complexity, tone, and whether the explanation is suitable for Grade 8.

Misconceptions

Whether a common student misunderstanding is addressed directly rather than merely avoided.

Format compliance

Word count, active voice, structure, and alignment with the content brief.
